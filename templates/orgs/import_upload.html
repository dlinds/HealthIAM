Column reference

Departments: code,name (aliases: department_code, dept_code, department_name)

Job codes: code,title (aliases: job_code, job_title, description)

Positions: department_code,job_code[,title] or position_code[,title] where position_code is DDDD-JJJJ

  • Header row required; comma, tab, semicolon, or pipe delimited; UTF-8.
  • Codes must be exactly four digits (keep leading zeros).
  • Rows are upserted by code. Records already present but inactive are reactivated.
  • Imported records are marked HR feed. With "deactivate missing" checked, HR-sourced records not in the file are inactivated; manual records are never touched.
